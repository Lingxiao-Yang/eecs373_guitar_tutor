Motivation
No visual guidance in traditional guitar learning
Chord shapes are hard to memorize without instant feedback
Existing tools are fixed to one instrument — not portable
System architecture
The guitar pickup signal is conditioned by the amplifier/filter AFE before entering the STM32 ADC. The keypad uses row/column GPIO scan lines. The display uses SPI, the WS2813 LEDs use a timed LED data signal, and the speaker receives DAC audio output through its built-in amplifier.
